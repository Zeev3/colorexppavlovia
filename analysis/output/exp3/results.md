## Data preprocessing

Trials with a report of 0 (the scale's starting value, recorded when the participant clicked without moving onto the scale) were treated as missing responses and removed. Trials with RT < 200 ms or RT > 8000 ms were removed, followed by within-participant +/-2.5 SD trimming on RT and removal of trials whose absolute deviation from the objective mean exceeded 30 scale units. Participants retaining fewer than 85% of their trials, or whose Spearman correlation between objective mean and report was <= 0.3, were excluded.

- average condition: 50 started, 1 excluded for an incomplete session, 3 for low trial retention, 0 for not tracking the stimulus, leaving **46**.
- experience condition: 51 started, 1 excluded as a second session (already took part in the other condition), 3 excluded for an incomplete session, 6 for low trial retention, 3 for not tracking the stimulus, leaving **38**.

- average: N = 46, 4201 trials (M = 91.3 per participant), mean absolute error = 4.92 scale units.

## What lies behind an extreme report

Two ways of looking at the tails. **Fixed cutoff**: trials whose report crosses the exaggeration thresholds (< 14 or > 36), and the objective mean behind them. **Matched**: the most extreme 10% of responses per side *within each cell*, so the selection rate is the same at every noise level; the ratio |objective deviation| / |response deviation| says how much real signal backs a unit of reported extremity.

### Symmetric report windows

Trials where the report fell within +/-2 of 10 or of 40. Unlike the fixed cutoffs above, the two windows sit symmetrically about the scale centre, so the low and high tails are treated alike. This is the sensitive form of the reverse-slope question: does a given extreme report back less extreme reality as noise rises?

_Report window ~40 skipped: too few trials (smallest cell had 14, minimum 20)._

- reported ~10: objective mean = 18.32 (2), 17.57 (10), 17.51 (20).
  - Effect of array size: F(2, 646.33) = 3.04, p = .049.

  - 2 - 20: estimate = 0.816, p = .076.
  - 10 - 20: estimate = 0.058, p = .990.

A drop in the fixed version with a flat matched ratio means the tails moved because the whole response distribution compressed, not because extreme reports became less informative.
